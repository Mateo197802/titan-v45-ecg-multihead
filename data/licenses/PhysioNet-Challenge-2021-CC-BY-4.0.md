# PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2021 Citation And License

The rhythm manifests contain 63 Primary8 and 47 Primary6 diagnostic rows whose source labels use the `training/` directories for CPSC 2018, CPSC-Extra, Georgia, Ningbo, and PTB-XL. These path labels match the [PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2021 v1.0.3 training layout](https://physionet.org/content/challenge-2021/1.0.3/), which is CC BY 4.0 (DOI `10.13026/34va-7q14`).

Cite both the challenge article and release: Reyna, M. A. et al. Will Two Do? Varying Dimensions in Electrocardiography: The PhysioNet/Computing in Cardiology Challenge 2021. *Computing in Cardiology* 48 (2021), DOI `10.23919/CinC53138.2021.9662687`; PhysioNet version 1.0.3 (2022), DOI `10.13026/34va-7q14`.

The attribution is inferred from the row-manifest paths. The repository does not include an upstream file-level checksum comparison proving that each waveform came from that precise release. The Challenge 2021 dataset page documents these training directories and its CC BY 4.0 terms. See `rhythm-source-attribution-v0.1.0.json`.
