\textbf{Evidence boundary.} Direct supervision means a documented objective in the recovered stage, not a fully authenticated training roster or complete pretraining history. Global-logit presence does not establish direct label exposure, a specialist decision or clinical validation. NSR is normal rhythm. The P6/P8 residual unions and the ALMI/ILMI/LAO--LAE pathology union are distinct experimental endpoints; their results do not validate every component individually. No published all-class Other specialist is established. Unrelated 41M experiments are not attributed to the released 58.35M backbones.
