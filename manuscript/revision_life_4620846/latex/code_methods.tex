\subsection{Patient-held-out evaluation within CODE after supervised adaptation}
\label{sec:code-methods}
CODE-15\% version 1.0.0 is a source external to the original TITAN experiments, with 345,779 ECGs from 233,770 patients in the provider release \citep{CODE15Dataset}. The completed analysis reported here is a patient-disjoint evaluation within CODE after supervised domain adaptation. The candidate used CODE training labels; its reserved CODE evaluation therefore does not constitute independent institutional confirmation of the adapted model. It is separate from the historical P6/P8 selected development profiles and from the unfinished independent study.

Patient hashes assigned 70/10/10/10\% buckets before caps and quality control (QC), with at most two ECGs per patient. The admitted partitions are reported in Table~\ref{tab:code-cohort}; their final sizes need not preserve the initial bucket proportions. The sealed audit reports zero patient-key overlap across these four partitions. Validation, calibration and test excluded patients meeting the earlier PR~6 selection predicate. The 992 training patients flagged by that predicate are not a verified intersection with the final historical PR~6 roster. Exact observed float32 waveform hashes were screened within CODE. Across all four partitions, QC excluded 41 all-zero, 745 ambiguous-duration, 52 flat-lead, 10 cross-split exact-waveform and 127 within-split duplicate observations. These pooled exclusion counts are not test-only counts. They do not establish absence of cross-institution duplicates or authenticate all historical TITAN training patients.

The source HDF5 stores 4,096 samples per lead at 400~Hz in the order I, II, III, aVR, aVL, aVF and V1--V6. The provider describes zero padding for variable-length ECGs \citep{CODE15Dataset}; the present admitted-window audit specifically found symmetric 581/581 all-lead zero borders, leaving 2,934 observed samples (7.335~s), and 0/0 or 48/48 border patterns, for which the center 4,000 samples were used. Other patterns were excluded. Observed nonzero extent is not a measurement of device acquisition duration. The selected pipeline applies third-order Butterworth second-order-section zero-phase filtering at 0.05--45~Hz, polyphase resampling to 125~Hz and per-lead z-score normalization. Length-grouped inputs have 917 or 1,250 samples, without synthetic padding entering the candidate. Physical acquisition gain is not inferred from HDF5 amplitude. This contract differs from the reconstructed causal 0.5--45~Hz, fixed-window original path.

The adapted candidate initializes the V3F residual CNN--Transformer backbone and adds a five-output multilayer perceptron with LayerNorm, 128 hidden units, SiLU and dropout. Its inputs comprise the pooled embedding, seven observed-window descriptors and two duration indicators. This head is distinct from the original 512-hidden-unit specialists and their ten morphology features. Training uses CODE train only, masked weighted binary cross-entropy, patient weighting and regularization toward the initial backbone. The last residual block, token projection, last two Transformer layers, final normalization and new head comprise 16,992,199 trainable parameters. The selected checkpoint is seed 20261112, epoch 5, job 33696. The median validation-average-precision seed of the eligible family with highest mean validation average precision was sealed before test scoring. Other source/self-supervised or loss variants were not selected.

Provider flags AF, SB, ST, RBBB and 1dAVb map to AFIB, SB, STACH, RBBB and 1AVB. Unrecognized values are masked; each final endpoint has 4,921 usable test references. These are provider reference flags without new clinical adjudication; AFIB denotes an ECG-associated statement. PVC, NSR, PAC and the P4 pathology statements are not evaluated by this five-endpoint comparison. Candidate thresholds, in the stated order, are 0.66, 0.87, 0.48, 0.44 and 0.51, fitted on calibration only over the 0.05--0.95 grid to maximize F1 with sensitivity floors of 0.50 for AFIB, RBBB and 1AVB. No post-hoc probability mapping was selected. Frozen P6/P8 weights and published class cuts were evaluated on the same observed ECGs, but their feature and probability paths differ. The comparison concerns complete operating systems, without isolating an effect of weights, descriptors, filtering or threshold adaptation alone.

All macro quantities average the same five binary endpoints equally. Average precision is non-interpolated; Brier and ECE follow the earlier definitions, with ten equal-width ECE bins. Uncertainty uses 1,000 patient-cluster bootstrap draws (seed 20261112), retaining all ECGs of each sampled patient and using identical draws for paired contrasts. The intervals condition on the selected checkpoint, thresholds and dataset. A stratum requires at least 20 positive and 20 negative patients per endpoint. Long-window SB fails this support rule, so duration-stratum macro values use different endpoint rosters and cannot be interpreted as a duration effect.

The public evidence snapshot is pinned to commit \texttt{63c74b3fde2c51f7f8cd}\allowbreak\texttt{e4ad396d77a1bd16cb29}. Jobs 33768 and 33769 respectively scored the frozen systems and reduced the sealed predictions; job 33772 independently read back those same private predictions. Its receipt reports 546 point comparisons (maximum absolute discrepancy $2.22\times10^{-16}$) and 30 macro-F1 interval-bound comparisons (zero discrepancy). This is an implementation readback, not an independent clinical cohort. The supplied public package permits hash-chain verification and recalculation of confusion-derived metrics and arithmetic macro averages; individual-score AUROC, average precision and bootstrap intervals are retained as authenticated aggregate results rather than claimed as locally recomputed from private scores.
