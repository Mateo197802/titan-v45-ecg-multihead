# PTB-XL Primary4 External-Development Subset

## Source

PTB-XL version 1.0.3, PhysioNet. Wagner et al., *Scientific Data* 7, 154 (2020). License: CC BY 4.0.

## Released Scope

The Release archive contains every fold-10 record referenced by the four promoted high-confidence Primary4 binary panels, not the complete PTB-XL dataset. Panel sizes are ASMI 294, LVH 224, IMI 206, and ISC_ 238 record evaluations.

## Processing And Limitations

Signals are resampled to 125 Hz and evaluated as `12 x 1250` windows. A record can participate in more than one classwise panel. Selection and repeated inspection make this external-development evidence.
