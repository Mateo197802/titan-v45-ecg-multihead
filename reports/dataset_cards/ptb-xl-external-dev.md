# PTB-XL Primary4 External-Development Subset

## Source

PTB-XL version 1.0.3, PhysioNet. Wagner et al., *Scientific Data* 7, 154 (2020). License: CC BY 4.0.

## Released Scope

The Release archive contains the fold-10 records referenced by the four high-confidence Primary4 binary panels. Panel sizes are ASMI 294, LVH 224, IMI 206, and ISC_ 238 record evaluations.

## Processing And Limitations

Signals are resampled to 125 Hz and evaluated as `12 x 1250` windows. A record can participate in more than one classwise panel. The cohort report lists the records, labels, predictions, and per-panel counts used by the published metrics.
