# No Clinical Use

TITAN V4.5 is an experimental research artifact. It is not a medical device and has not been validated for diagnosis, treatment, triage, monitoring, or patient management.

Primary8 did not pass its declared accuracy gate. Primary6 is a six-class diagnostic view and must not be reported as Primary8. Primary4 was accepted by project decision at its observed metrics; it did not pass the historical accuracy gate. Repeated external evaluation makes the released cohorts external-development evidence, not untouched external-final validation.

Uncertainty estimates and quarantine rules can flag selected high-uncertainty predictions. They do not guarantee correctness and do not prevent hallucinations or unsafe clinical use.
