No clinical adjudication or absence of historical pretraining overlap is inferred.

## A defensible next institution

[Harvard-Emory ECG Database v5.0](https://bdsp.io/content/heedb/5.0/) is a candidate for confirmation on the Emory site (I0006). Its documented ECG-level `codes_physician` overreads, linked patient keys and 10-second waveforms permit a candidate label and signal audit without treating encounter ICD diagnoses as waveform truth. Access requires credentialing, the provider's required training and a signed DUA. Access for this project and storage/compute conditions were not established. No files were downloaded, outcomes opened, or code mappings assumed. For a future release, check authorized overread coverage, positive and explicit control support, exact onset/location meaning, history/uncertainty, and overlap before freezing the analysis. An overread is not automatically an adjudicated outcome.

[LUDB](https://physionet.org/content/ludb/1.0.1/) is open and includes diagnostic annotations, but its public supports of four STACH, ten first-degree AV block, and four complete RBBB ECGs do not meet the existing 20-patient minimum for a five-endpoint confirmation. Incomplete RBBB cannot fill complete-RBBB support. It is useful for technical checks, not for lowering the gate.

For P4, assess HEEDB physician overread codes individually. No ASMI/LVH/IMI/ISC_ mapping or valid negative pool is established. PTB Diagnostic is an infarction-localization candidate with historical exposure concerns, not a replacement for independent P4 confirmation. Do not substitute ICD, unmentioned findings, or nonspecific ST-T changes for verified P4 labels.
