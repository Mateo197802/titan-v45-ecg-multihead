## Cohort decisions before performance

- [LUDB v1.0.1 and README](https://physionet.org/content/ludb/1.0.1/):
  open ODC Attribution license, 200 waveforms and explicitly documented
  source subjects, publisher SHA-256 manifest and principal rhythm categories.
  Use the full release. Main external-transport test of the frozen policy
  without fitting; historical independence is a separate exposure audit.
  Subject and signal links remain private. A narrow label audit is not a
  patient-level clinical adjudication study.
- [CODE-test](https://zenodo.org/records/3765780) is not a new confirmatory
  cohort: its version is named in released rhythm manifests. Existing
  [CODE-15%](https://zenodo.org/records/4916206) results are a distinct
  previously scored study with limited endpoint annotations; retain their
  original scope rather than reusing it as an untouched test.
- [MIMIC-IV-ECG](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-ecg/1.0/) has
  source subject linkage and machine reports; the existing CEDIA
  development audit found no endpoints with sufficient explicit negatives
  under its frozen reference contract. Do not unseal its retained
  references or reinterpret silence as a negative. The
  [Harvard-Emory release](https://bdsp.io/content/heedb/5.0/) requires
  credentials, approved training and a DUA; access has not been verified
  for this study.
- [CODE-II](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-026-02704-4) is a follow-up
  candidate for broader labels, but the authors describe controlled
  assessment of blinded annotations. Do not submit individual predictions
  externally or assume availability from publication text.
- [PTB Diagnostic v1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/ptbdb/1.0.0/)
  is a clinically enriched potential complementary source, not an
  all-comers cohort. Separate it from PTB-XL and audit provider, exposure
  and endpoint-specific references before authorizing any inference.

## Execution and reporting boundary

Score first using the hash-pinned released assets, native preprocessing and
thresholds, in a private CEDIA allocation. Join references only after the
ledger and routes are sealed. No calibration, selection, training or endpoint
threshold changes on LUDB. Keep the complete denominator even if no reference
metric is estimable for a class. Validate provider archive integrity, score and
reference key sets, hashes, route algebra, confusion denominators and split
overlap before exporting any aggregates. Declare clinically unsupported
requirements explicitly, even if the technical jobs are green.

Use a new draft PR based on the November experiment branch, not any existing
manuscript or reviewer PR. Neither the article, abstract, figures, conclusions,
letter, released weights, PR #3/#4/#5/#7/#8 nor historical scoring is edited.
