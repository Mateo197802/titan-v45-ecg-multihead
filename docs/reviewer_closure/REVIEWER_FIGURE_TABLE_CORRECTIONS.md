## Definitions and abbreviations to standardize

- **P6 / Primary6:** six rhythm classes: AFIB, SB, STACH, RBBB, 1AVB and PVC; diagnostic profile, not Primary8 performance.
- **P8 / Primary8:** eight rhythm classes: AFIB, SB, STACH, NSR, RBBB, PAC, 1AVB and PVC; current project status remains candidate/does not pass the complete gate.
- **P4 / Primary4:** four pathology labels: ASMI, LVH, IMI and ISC_; selected panel result, project accepted by decision, not an independent clinical validation.
- **ECG statement:** a code recorded by the source for an ECG. Statement absence is not an adjudicated negative or absence of disease.
- **Exact-waveform duplicate group:** identical waveform fingerprints under the frozen matching rule. Do not equate exact duplicate screening with cross-institution patient identity or approximate-duplicate exclusion.
- **Empty threshold set:** no class crossed its threshold. **P8 fallback:** forced top-1 decision when the set is empty. Neither means quarantine, `Other`, referral or human review.
- **Quarantine:** only a disposition generated by a frozen uncertainty policy and ledger. Its rate is `not assessed` when policy, scores and denominator are unavailable.
